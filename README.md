# LIBAC: annotated battery literature for NLP

Research publication by **Hassna El-Bousiydy and collaborators** · Publication 03 of 03

## Publication

**LIBAC: An Annotated Corpus for Automated “Reading” of the Lithium-Ion Battery Research Literature**

Hassna El-Bousiydy, Javier F. Troncoso, Patrik Johansson and Alejandro A. Franco.

*Chemistry of Materials*, 35(5), 1849–1857 (2023). First published online: **21 February 2023**; journal issue: **14 March 2023**.

**[Read the article and find its supporting information →](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemmater.2c01356)**

## Research overview

Extracting structured facts from scientific papers requires examples that connect domain language to meaningful entity categories. LIBAC addresses this need with an annotated corpus for lithium-ion battery literature and an evaluation of named-entity recognition models.

The publication reports:

- **6,985 paragraphs** with **28 entity categories**.
- **73,300 annotated spans** across **627,428 tokens**.
- Reported F1 scores of **81%** for a Tok2vec-based model and **83%** for a Transformer-based model.

These are the study's reported evaluation results, not performance guarantees for other datasets or deployed systems.
